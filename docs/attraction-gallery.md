# See the place, then hear its story

Rails & Roots is a proposed guide. These sights suggest **future local visits** along a simulated historical rail route; the photographs are not proof that trains stop there today, that sights are visible from a train, or that a traveller can walk to them from a station. For the five featured locations, the image leads into a longer story. Supporting places get a shorter story and an honest invitation to explore.

Images in this gallery load from their credited hosts when online. To make an offline app, first obtain local copies under each image’s terms and commit them to the appropriate image folder. The existing Pretoria and Johannesburg station photos in the repository illustrate rail context; the attraction choices below are the proposed destination covers.

## Five featured destinations

### Pretoria: Union Buildings

[![Union Buildings — Ruby D-Brown](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Union_Buildings_Pretoria_12.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Union_Buildings_Pretoria_12.jpg)

This civic landmark introduces the capital before Lerato boards Gautrain; the image is a separate city excursion. Photo: [Ruby D-Brown](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Union_Buildings_Pretoria_12.jpg), CC BY 4.0.

### Johannesburg: Constitution Hill

[![Constitution Hill — ConstHill](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/ConstitutionHill.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ConstitutionHill.jpg)

The former prison complex and constitutional site helps tell Johannesburg’s human story; it is not the Park Station interchange. Photo: [ConstHill](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ConstitutionHill.jpg), CC BY-SA 3.0.

### Kimberley: The Big Hole

[![The Big Hole — Entropy1963](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Kimberley_Big_Hole.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kimberley_Big_Hole.jpg)

A vast former diamond mine opens the story of labour, mineral wealth and local memory. Photo: [Entropy1963](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kimberley_Big_Hole.jpg), Public domain.

### Beaufort West: Karoo National Park

[![Karoo National Park — South African Tourism](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Karoo_National_Park_-_Central_Karoo,_South_Africa_(3883080917).jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Karoo_National_Park_-_Central_Karoo,_South_Africa_(3883080917).jpg)

The park’s Karoo landscape is a separately planned excursion from Beaufort West. Photo: [South African Tourism](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Karoo_National_Park_-_Central_Karoo,_South_Africa_(3883080917).jpg), CC BY 2.0.

### Cape Town: Table Mountain

[![Table Mountain — Romaine](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Cape_Town_-_Table_Mountain_seen_from_Waterfront_(2).jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cape_Town_-_Table_Mountain_seen_from_Waterfront_(2).jpg)

The mountain is a city landmark, viewed from the Waterfront; choose a separate walk or sightseeing visit. Photo: [Romaine](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cape_Town_-_Table_Mountain_seen_from_Waterfront_(2).jpg), CC0 1.0.

## Supporting destinations

Each supporting stop has a JSON file in `data/stops/`. The [full route gallery](stops-and-images.md) shows the selected sight, story lead and route order. The [image ledger](../assets/images/README.md) contains creator, licence and subject information.

**Christiana:** [an early morning photograph of the Vaal River](https://www.clickchristiana.co.za/moderne-christiana-gallery.php) is credited there to Dr Robert Boizard. Click Christiana publicly lists [info@clickchristiana.co.za](mailto:info@clickchristiana.co.za). Ask the site to connect the team with the photographer for written reuse permission; until then the photograph appears as a link only, not an embedded picture or repository asset.

**Hutchinson:** the heritage-building photograph is of Victoria West, reached by a separate road trip. **Krugersdorp:** the waterfall is in Roodepoort and also requires a separate excursion. **Bloemhof:** the dam photo was taken on the Free State shore, while the suggested nature reserve is on the North West shore. **Warrenton:** the river sunset is from the N12 north of town, not a photo of its art centre.
