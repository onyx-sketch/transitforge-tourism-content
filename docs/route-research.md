# Route research and decisions

**Research checked:** 23 September 2026

## Why Lerato changes trains in Johannesburg

The demonstration begins in Pretoria. Gautrain connects Pretoria with its Park Station in Johannesburg. Gautrain describes that station as adjacent to Johannesburg Park Station. The historically documented Shosholoza Meyl Johannesburg–Cape Town journey began at Johannesburg Park Station.

The interchange joins two parts of the *story*. It is not a single train, ticket or guaranteed connection.

A team map search between the selected Gautrain Park Station and Johannesburg Park Station address pins displayed approximately 500 metres and six minutes on foot. The result does not verify the exact station exits or time between platforms.

## Historical station order

The Competition Commission records Johannesburg Park Station, Krugersdorp, Potchefstroom, Klerksdorp, Bloemhof, Christiana, Warrenton, Kimberley, De Aar, Hutchinson, Beaufort West, Laingsburg, Matjiesfontein, Worcester, Wellington, Huguenot, Bellville and Cape Town.

The report misspells Laingsburg and Bellville; the route data uses their standard spellings. All 18 names remain in the route record. Featured content can be deeper without deleting the other stops.

## What the prototype can say

Rails & Roots uses a simulated long-distance journey to help travellers discover South African places. It can suggest places for a future domestic trip. It cannot claim that this prototype has already generated visits or that its simulated train can currently be booked.
